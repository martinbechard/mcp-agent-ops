# Product Requirements Document

## Purpose and audience

### Purpose

Explain why the product change is needed and the outcome it must create.

### Intended readers

Identify the people who approve, implement, operate, and verify the change.

## Scope

### In scope

Define the capabilities that the document covers.

### Out of scope

Record the related capabilities that the delivery does not include.

## User experience

### Primary workflow

Describe the actor's path from entry to a successful outcome.

### Accessibility

State the keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, and responsive expectations.

## Delivery

### Milestones

#### Discovery

Confirm the problem, actors, constraints, and acceptance signals.

#### Implementation

Build the approved scope and retain evidence for each completed behavior.

#### Release

Verify the release candidate and monitor the staged rollout.

### Risks and mitigations

Document each material delivery risk with its owner and response.

## Verification

### Acceptance criteria

List observable conditions that determine whether the feature is complete.

### Test evidence

Connect each acceptance condition to automated or manual evidence.
